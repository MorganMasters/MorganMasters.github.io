Named a **USDA Agricultural Experiment Station Fellow** for a second consecutive year, supporting field-robotics research at UC Santa Cruz.

The fellowship funds the work behind BirdsEye: UAV-based sensing and geospatial annotation aimed at making AI-driven crop and rangeland monitoring affordable for small and mid-scale growers, and at persistently monitoring low-power wireless sensor arrays in the field.
